Pro-Tips for Implementing This
Go with CSV: For admin tools, CSVs are usually the easiest way to handle bulk data because you can scrape lists like the one you found, drop them into Excel or Google Sheets, format the columns, and export directly.

Validation is Key: The Pydantic model on your FastAPI backend is your best friend here. It will stop bad data (like text in a date field) from crashing your Supabase database.

Keep it Simple First: Don't worry about complex AI-matching context generation in the bulk upload initially. Just get the raw data in, and you can always build an "AI Auto-Fill" button for individual records later.
